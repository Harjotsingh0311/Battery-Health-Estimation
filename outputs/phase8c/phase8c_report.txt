## Suggested limitations text
- Online references differ from the stored ones in ['b1c18', 'b1c41']; these cells contribute 5 of the 14 cycles with |d| > 0.5 pp (36%). Parity statistics should be quoted with and without them.
- Forced stored references in b1c18: median |d| 0.090 -> 0.015 pp (p99 0.343 -> 0.169 pp), so the reference effect on the bulk is confirmed; a tail remains (max 0.795 pp) that the references do not explain.
- Forced stored references in b1c41: median |d| 0.055 -> 0.017 pp (p99 0.140 -> 0.069 pp), so the reference effect on the bulk is confirmed.
- Top 30 differences in development cells, closeness to soh_nom: 20 online closer to truth, 9 stored closer to truth, 1 similar (within 0.1 pp).
- Typical agreement: median |d| 0.0346 pp, median signed d +0.0295 pp, online above offline; excluding b1c18 p99 |d| 0.1803 pp and 0.099% of cycles above 0.5 pp.
- Temperature (observational; two episodes in two cells, not a calibrated sensitivity, correlation not proof; M0 has no temperature input; figures from the v2 run, S4a/S4c): b3c38 cycles 1321-1329: average temperature rises from 35.6 to 37.0 C (minimum 32.0 to 33.6 C), flagged as jumps above 3x local variation, internal resistance unchanged (0.0162 to 0.0160); the estimate steps up by about 0.8 pp while the true SOH is smooth (about 0.6 pp per C). b1c36 cycles 489-493: average temperature falls from 34.4 to 28.9 C (minimum 25.8 C) with an internal-resistance jump, recovering at cycle 493; the estimate dips to 93.7 and 92.9 pp against a true 94.9 and 94.7 (about 0.2-0.3 pp per C). A measurement across all development cells would settle whether temperature drives these artifacts.
- Unresolved isolated case, b1c5 cycle 158: online 92.3 pp, offline 98.9 pp, true 98.0 pp; the online window differs from the stored one by 0.143 Ah, largest at grid index 641 (V about 2.54 V); one cycle in 10,744, cause unresolved. Sample-gap check (S3e): before the estimate time the largest time step is 5.01 s (median step 5.00 s) and the largest voltage step 353.6 mV, against control maxima of 5.03 s and 15.7 mV (n=56); the cycle is at control percentile 57 (time step) and 100 (voltage step), above the control range, so a sampling gap is not excluded. The earlier hypothesis that upward voltage steps latched by the running minimum cause the isolated spikes is refuted (largest upward step 0.0 mV in the 20 worst cycles).
- The compared rows are scored2 cycles, so offline-flagged cycles are mostly absent by construction; flags do not explain the tail.
- Flag definition, reference-difference rule, closeness band (0.1 pp), jump rule (3x robust sigma), dip rule and tolerances are my choices and were not tuned on labels. E1p, E3p and E4p were written after the first results were seen; E5 and E6 were registered before the rerun after v4. The temperature figures above are quoted from the v2 run.
